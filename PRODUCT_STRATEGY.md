# 🎯 Praman AI: Feasibility, Viability & Strategic Roadmap

> **The New Category**: **Causal Crisis Intelligence (CCI)**.
> Praman AI doesn't just predict *that* a crisis will happen; it identifies the **Levers of Change** to prevent it.

---

## 1. 🏗️ Feasibility Analysis (Can we build it?)

### **Technical Feasibility**
*   **Data Liquidity**: We leverage high-frequency satellite data (Open-Meteo, NASA POWER) and validated humanitarian APIs (UNHCR, World Bank). The "data skeleton" exists and is accessible via modern APIs.
*   **Causal Infrastructure**: The shift from Black-Box ML to **Structural Causal Models (SCM)** is now technically viable thanks to libraries like `DoWhy` and `CausalML`. We are no longer limited by correlation.
*   **Scalability**: Built on Next.js and Python (FastAPI), the system is cloud-native and can scale from a single region (Ethiopia) to global coverage without architectural redesign.

### **Operational Feasibility**
*   **Expert Integration**: Praman AI allows "Human-in-the-loop" causal discovery, where domain experts can validate or adjust the graph, making it operationally trustworthy for high-stakes decisions.

## 2. 💰 Viability Analysis (Is it a business?)

### **The Market Gap**
*   **The Climate Paradox**: Bilateral donors spend **$30B+ annually** on climate adaptation, yet **60% is misdirected** because they lack evidence of *which* intervention causes *what* outcome. 
*   **Economic Impact**: By identifying that "Conflict" is a bigger driver than "Drought" in a specific week, Praman AI can save agencies millions in inefficient aid delivery.

### **The Revenue Model**
*   **B2G (Business-to-Government)**: Licensing for National Disaster Management Authorities (NDMAs).
*   **B2B (Multilaterals)**: Enterprise SaaS for the UN, World Bank, and Red Cross.
*   **Insurance/Reinsurance**: Providing "Causal Risk Audit" for पैरामीट्रिक (parametric) insurance payouts.
